## Features

- **Lillie as your partner:** she follows you through Alola, and you can talk to her (press **A** when the
  prompt shows over her head). She's a different Lillie this time, dressed in pink.
- **The Ultra Wormhole roguelike:** a wormhole in Iki Town leads to a five-room run. Each room has a boss,
  Lillie hands out power-ups before every room, and an Ultra Beast waits at the end.
- **Star Bits and Ultra levels:** win battles to earn Star Bits, and push your Pokémon past their limits.
- **A new difficulty system** (in progress).

## Quick Start

### 1. What you need

- **Pokémon Ultra Sun**, your own copy, with its **1.2 update** installed. The mod only works with version 1.2.
- One of:
  - the **Azahar** emulator (a recent version), or
  - a **Nintendo 3DS** with **Luma3DS** (custom firmware).

### 2. [Download](../../releases/latest)

Get `UltraLillieMod-<version>-Setup.exe` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

### 3. Install

#### Windows: Azahar

1. Close Azahar.
2. Run `Setup.exe`, pick **Azahar emulator**, and click **Install**.
3. Start Pokémon Ultra Sun in Azahar.

#### Windows: Nintendo 3DS

1. Turn the 3DS off and put its SD card in your PC.
2. Run `Setup.exe`, pick **Nintendo 3DS (SD card)**, and click **Install**.
3. Put the card back. The first time: hold **SELECT** while turning the 3DS on, tick
   **Enable game patching**, and press **START** to save.
4. Start Pokémon Ultra Sun.

> [!NOTE]
> Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed. Click **More info** →
> **Run anyway**.

To remove the mod, run `Setup.exe` again and click **Uninstall**.

### 4. Play!

Start a new game or load your save. Lillie joins you, and the wormhole waits in Iki Town.

## Back up your save

- **Azahar:** right-click Pokémon Ultra Sun → **Open Save Data Location**, and copy that folder somewhere safe.
- **3DS:** back up your save with your usual save manager before installing.

The mod stores Star Bits in your Bag and Ultra levels in your Pokémon. Don't trade or move Pokémon from a
test save.

## Reporting bugs

Please include:
1. The mod version (in the installer's title bar and the release name).
2. Whether you play on Azahar or a 3DS.
3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
4. A screenshot or short video if you can. If the game crashed on Azahar, its newest log file
   (Help → Open Log Folder).

## Project overview

The mod is two small files: a code patch for the game's 1.2 executable and our own data. Everything it changes
is patched into the game's memory as the game runs, so nothing of the game itself is ever shipped. A legally
acquired copy of Pokémon Ultra Sun is required.
